KING PETS - PRIVACY NOTICE
(GDPR - Regulation (EU) 2016/679 - and ePrivacy information)
Version 1.0.1 - applies to King Animated Desktop 1.0.x

IN SHORT
King Animated Desktop does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data. It has no
accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no cookies and no internet
connection of its own. Everything stays on your computer. The only time personal data
reaches the Publisher is if YOU choose to donate or e-mail (sections 12 and 13).

1. WHO IS RESPONSIBLE
TeamExyKings, Chennai, India  (the "Publisher")
Contact for privacy questions: Yashwanthramsomireddy@gmail.com
Because the Publisher receives no personal data from the app, there is no
processing for which the Publisher acts as controller. This notice is provided
for transparency.

2. WHAT THE APP STORES ON YOUR COMPUTER (only on your device)
  a) Your settings: %APPDATA%\KingAnimatedDesktop\config.json
     (chosen objects, sizes, colours, badge text, etc.). Whatever you type as
     "badge text" is stored only in this file.
  b) A start-up entry, only if "Start with Windows" is on:
     HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Run  ->  KingAnimatedDesktop
  c) A temporary lock file (kingdesktop.lock) in your temp folder, so that two copies
     do not run at once.
  d) Optional: settings files you choose to export yourself.
None of this is transmitted anywhere.

3. WHAT THE APP READS LOCALLY (and never stores or sends)
  - Size and window class of the foreground window - only to hide the pets during
    full-screen apps/games (setting "Hide while a full-screen app/game is active").
  - Power status (battery / mains) - only if you enable "Hide when running on
    battery".
  - The system clock - only if you enable "quiet hours".
  - Your monitor layout - to place the pets on screen.
  This information is used in memory for an instant and is discarded.

4. LEGAL BASIS
No personal data is processed by the Publisher. Local settings are created at
your request when you install and use the program (Art. 6(1)(b) / your own
device). Nothing requires consent under Art. 6(1)(a), and no cookies or similar
tracking technologies are used (ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC, Art. 5(3)).

5. RECIPIENTS AND INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS
None. No data leaves your device, so there are no recipients, processors or
transfers outside the EU/EEA.

6. UPDATES
King Animated Desktop never checks for updates by itself. New versions are only obtained if you
download them from https://github.com/yashwanthramsomireddy/king-animated-desktop/releases . Your
download is then subject to GitHub's own privacy policy, not this app's.

7. RETENTION AND ERASURE ("right to be forgotten")
Your data lives only on your PC. You can delete it at any time:
  - Uninstall King Animated Desktop and answer YES when asked "Also delete my settings?"
    (the uninstaller removes %APPDATA%\KingAnimatedDesktop and the start-up entry), or
  - delete the folder %APPDATA%\KingAnimatedDesktop yourself.
Turning off "Start with Windows" in Settings removes the start-up entry.

8. YOUR RIGHTS
Under GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction,
portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with your national
data protection authority. Because the Publisher holds no data about you, these
rights are exercised directly by you on your own device as described above. You
may also write to the contact address above with any question.

9. CHILDREN
The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

10. SECURITY
The program has no network functionality and no server component. It does not run
with elevated rights after installation. Install only from the official
repository/Releases page and check the installer's digital signature/checksum
where provided.

12. DONATIONS (OPTIONAL)
The project website (teamexykings.in) has PayPal and Razorpay donate links. They only open
the provider's payment page in your browser when you click. Donating is voluntary and
unlocks nothing. The Publisher never sees your card/bank/UPI details. From the provider the
Publisher receives the amount, date, reference and the name/e-mail you gave them, used only
to acknowledge the donation and keep accounting records (legal basis: legitimate interest
Art. 6(1)(f) and legal obligation Art. 6(1)(c)); retained as long as Indian accounting/tax
rules require. PayPal (Luxembourg/USA) and Razorpay (India) may process data outside the
EU/EEA under their own safeguards and privacy policies.

13. LINKS AND E-MAIL
Buttons for the website, source code, Privacy policy and Terms of use only act when you
click them, and then open your own browser (the policy buttons open this project's GitHub
repository). The program itself has no "e-mail support" button; if you choose to e-mail the
Publisher at the contact address above, your address and message are used only to answer you.

14. CHANGES
If a future version ever changes how data is handled, this notice will be updated
and the change described in CHANGELOG.md before release.
